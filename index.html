Security Architecture for Certified Systems

I architected and implemented the security for Rockwell Collins' Aircraft Information Manager service and for its interface to the IMS-3500/6000 onboard equipment — the trust boundary where a ground system talks to certified avionics. There was no airworthiness security standard to follow at the time, so the design had to carry its own argument. That is exactly where AI governance sits today: no settled standard, and you still have to show why your controls are sufficient and produce the evidence that they held.
